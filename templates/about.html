How it works

A transparent risk check, not a verdict.

Important

This tool estimates risk from signals in the text you provide. A low score does not prove a job is genuine, and a high score does not prove it is fake. Always verify a company independently.

Two checks combined

  1. Rule-based checks: look for known warning signs such as fees, guaranteed-job claims, requests for OTPs, urgency and suspicious contact methods.
  2. Machine learning check: a TF-IDF + Logistic Regression model trained on public job postings looks at wording patterns.

Limitations

  • It only reads the text you paste. It does not visit websites or contact anyone.
  • Scammers change their wording, so new tricks may be missed.
  • Genuine postings can occasionally be flagged.
